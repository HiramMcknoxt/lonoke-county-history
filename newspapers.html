The county's newspapers

From the U.S. Newspaper Directory (Library of Congress) and the papers themselves. Still publishing means the paper is alive today; living papers are treated as partners, not scrape targets.

PaperTownYearsWhere the archive lives
The England Democrat Still publishing England1934–present Founded Nov. 1, 1934. The paper's office at 121 E. Haywood St. holds a copy of every back issue, and the building is being converted to include a newspaper museum. Microfilm at the regional library system. Now published by Newsroom Ventures (Roby Brock), at englanddemocrat.com. LCCN SN92050004.
The Carlisle Independent Still publishing Carlisle1905–present Part of the run is digitized and free on Chronicling America through the Arkansas Digital Newspaper Project (issues from at least 1918 are confirmed there). Microfilm at the regional library system. LCCN SN92050006.
The Lonoke Democrat (and predecessors) Lonoke1873– The county seat's lineage: Lonoke Weekly Democrat (1873–1907), the Lonoke Weekly Ledger, the Lonoke Weeky Democrat (1913), and The Lonoke Democrat (1908–1913; 1914– , final years still being pinned down). Microfilm held by the regional library system's genealogy program.
The Cabot Star-Herald and Cabot predecessors Cabot1885– The Cabot Guard (1885), the Cabot Herald, and the Star-Herald (1956– ) survive on microfilm at Lonoke County Libraries — covered in depth at The Cabot Lore Repository.
Arkansas Leader Still publishing Cabot–present 467 print issues (2017–2026) already collected by The Cabot Lore Repository; indexed as links and summaries.

The Lonoke County Lore Repository is a public, non-commercial history project. Its sister site, The Cabot Lore Repository, covers the city of Cabot in depth. In-copyright newspapers are indexed as links, citations, and summaries — full text appears only for public-domain or permitted material.
