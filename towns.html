The county, town by town

Each community gets a page as its material is collected. The paper of record is the starting point for each; the last column notes what the Photographs page holds for each place today.

CommunityPaper of recordStatus
CabotStar-Herald · Arkansas Leader Deep collection lives at The Cabot Lore Repository; Camp Nelson, at Cabot's edge, is in this site's gallery.
Lonoke (county seat)Lonoke Democrat lineage Microfilm located; indexing queue. Gallery: the courthouse, the lone oak, the Democrat office, the depot, the rice mill — plus Eberts Field and the Joe Hogan Fish Hatchery.
EnglandThe England Democrat Complete back-issue archive at the paper's office; partnership outreach planned. Gallery: Church Street c.1910, cotton at the depot 1908, the Democrat office today, and Dorothea Lange's 1938 photographs from just outside town.
CarlisleThe Carlisle Independent Digitized issues on Chronicling America; first collection target. Gallery: rice trucks downtown 1945, the Dairyman's Bank, the cheese factory, the depot, Main Street.
Scott— (England Democrat country) Gallery: Plum Bayou Mounds, Marlsgate and the Dortch Plantation, All Souls Church, Cotham's Mercantile, the depot, and the Plantation Agriculture Museum.
Ward— (served by Cabot/Lonoke papers) Gallery: Busby Lake, street scenes, the municipal building, the Methodist church.
Austin & Old Austin— Gallery: Austin's last early-1900s building, city hall, the post office — and Old Austin's Sears House, cemetery, and Trail of Tears marker.
Keo— (England Democrat country) Gallery: the Keo Commercial Historic District, a late-1800s street scene, the 1909 United Methodist Church.
Coy · Humnoke · Tomberlin— Gallery: Coy's abandoned depot and water tower, Humnoke's post office and street scenes, Tomberlin's streets and its 1908 mock-funeral photograph.
Brownsville— (Prairie County seat before 1873) Gallery: the Centennial marker, the 1863 skirmish memorial, the Baptist church and cemetery.
Allport · Pettus · Furlow · Slovak— No photograph found yet for Allport — it is the collection's most wanted picture. These are exactly the places the county papers' community columns covered; contributions are how they will be filled in.

The Lonoke County Lore Repository is a public, non-commercial history project. Its sister site, The Cabot Lore Repository, covers the city of Cabot in depth. In-copyright newspapers are indexed as links, citations, and summaries — full text appears only for public-domain or permitted material. Photographs are shown with their credits; any credited holder can have a photograph removed on request.
