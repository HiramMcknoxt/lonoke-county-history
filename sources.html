Where the record lives

Lonoke / Prairie Regional Library System — genealogy program

The single richest confirmed holding: the system's historical archives focus on Lonoke and Prairie Counties and include microfilm of the Lonoke Democrat, the Carlisle Independent, and the England Democrat, plus Lonoke County cemetery records. Resident genealogist Leisa Horness is based at the Arlene Cherry Memorial Library in Cabot (Mon–Tue 8–5, Wed 8–12) · 1 (855) 572-6657 ext. 102.

The England Democrat office, England

A bound copy of every back issue since 1934, kept at 121 E. Haywood St., where a historic newspaper museum is being built into the renovated office. The paper's front-page Yesteryear column is drawn from this archive.

Lonoke County Library System

Holds the Cabot Star-Herald microfilm (in-person, Cabot branch). A digitization inquiry covering the Star-Herald run was sent November 4, 2026 and now serves both repositories.

Arkansas State Archives — Arkansas Digital Newspaper Project

The Archives hold roughly 3,000 Arkansas newspaper titles and run the state's National Digital Newspaper Program work: 300,000+ pages digitized from 25+ counties, free on Chronicling America — including part of the Carlisle Independent.

Chronicling America (Library of Congress)

chroniclingamerica.loc.gov — free searchable page images, and the U.S. Newspaper Directory behind the inventory on this site.

University of Arkansas Libraries

A sortable spreadsheet of Arkansas newspapers on microfilm (by title, county, and city) — the cross-check for what exists on film statewide.

The Lonoke County Lore Repository is a public, non-commercial history project. Its sister site, The Cabot Lore Repository, covers the city of Cabot in depth. In-copyright newspapers are indexed as links, citations, and summaries — full text appears only for public-domain or permitted material.
