About this project

The Lonoke County Lore Repository is the county-wide sibling of The Cabot Lore Repository. Cabot's site proved the model in one city — council records, both newspapers, photographs, oral histories, and a door for residents to contribute. This site runs the same model for the whole county, with the area newspapers as its backbone.

The rules it runs on

— Public records in full where the law allows. Copyrighted newspapers as links, citations, and summaries — pointers, not copies.
— Every claim carries its source; gaps are named, not hidden.
— Nothing is published from a collection batch without review.
— Living newspapers are partners. The England Democrat and Carlisle Independent are still publishing; their archives are theirs, and any bulk use needs their agreement.
— Photographs carry their credits, and any credited holder can have a photograph removed on request.

Contributing

The contribution door is open: the Contribute page links the form for photos, documents, recordings, and memories — with the same permission and credit system as Cabot's — and the Oral History guide shows you how to record the older people in your life before their stories are gone. Family papers, school annuals, church histories, and photographs from any Lonoke County community are all in scope.

The Lonoke County Lore Repository is a public, non-commercial history project. Its sister site, The Cabot Lore Repository, covers the city of Cabot in depth. In-copyright newspapers are indexed as links, citations, and summaries — full text appears only for public-domain or permitted material. Photographs are shown with their credits; any credited holder can have a photograph removed on request.
