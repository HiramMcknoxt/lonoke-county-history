About this project

The Lonoke County Lore Repository is the county-wide sibling of The Cabot Lore Repository. Cabot's site proved the model in one city — council records, both newspapers, photographs, oral histories, and a door for residents to contribute. This site runs the same model for the whole county, with the area newspapers as its backbone.

The rules it runs on

— Public records in full where the law allows. Copyrighted newspapers as links, citations, and summaries — pointers, not copies.
— Every claim carries its source; gaps are named, not hidden.
— Nothing is published from a collection batch without review.
— Living newspapers are partners. The England Democrat and Carlisle Independent are still publishing; their archives are theirs, and any bulk use needs their agreement.

Contributing

A contribution door (photos, documents, recordings, memories — with the same permission and credit system as Cabot's) is planned. Until it opens, the fastest way to help is to tell us what you have: family papers, school annuals, church histories, and photographs from any Lonoke County community are all in scope.

The Lonoke County Lore Repository is a public, non-commercial history project. Its sister site, The Cabot Lore Repository, covers the city of Cabot in depth. In-copyright newspapers are indexed as links, citations, and summaries — full text appears only for public-domain or permitted material.
