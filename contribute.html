Contribute to the Repository

This archive grows one piece at a time, and some of the best pieces are sitting in attics, albums, and desk drawers from Lonoke to Keo. If you have a piece of Lonoke County history, you can share it here.

What we're looking for

  • Photographs — streets, schools, teams, churches, businesses, farms, events, buildings, or just everyday county life, any era, any community. Allport, in particular, has no photograph in this collection yet.
  • Documents — programs, letters, maps, menus, tickets, flyers, reports, yearbook pages, anything printed or written about Lonoke County.
  • Newspaper clippings — especially from the England Democrat, Carlisle Independent, and Lonoke Democrat runs that survive on microfilm or in bound volumes.
  • Written memories — a paragraph about a place, a person, or an event is a contribution too. Type it into the form, or upload it as a document.
  • Oral histories — a recorded conversation with someone who remembers the county that is gone. A phone recording is fine. New to interviewing? Read Collecting an Oral History, our short guide to interviewing the older people in your life — then upload the recording the same way.

How it works

  1. Upload through the form below — the file, your name, and whatever you can tell us about it (the form asks which community it belongs to).
  2. Review. Nothing is posted automatically. A person reads every submission first, and we email you with any questions.
  3. Indexing and posting. Approved items are indexed — the text is extracted so they become searchable — and posted with credit to you, exactly as you specify on the form (your name, or anonymous). Oral-history recordings are transcribed first, and the transcript is what gets indexed.

Please only share material you have the right to share. Scans or clear photos of documents are perfect — keep your originals. Depending on copyright, some contributed items (newspaper clippings in particular) may be indexed and described rather than reproduced in full.

Open the contribution form (opens in Google Forms; Google requires sign-in to upload a file).

The Lonoke County Lore Repository is a public, non-commercial history project. Its sister site, The Cabot Lore Repository, covers the city of Cabot in depth. In-copyright newspapers are indexed as links, citations, and summaries — full text appears only for public-domain or permitted material. Photographs are shown with their credits; any credited holder can have a photograph removed on request.
